# Code of Conduct

Codara Studio follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep discussions about the work, and do not harass anyone. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at info@codarasolutions.com. Reports are handled privately; the maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban a participant depending on severity.
